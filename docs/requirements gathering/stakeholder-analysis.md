**Responsible:** Mustafa Mahmoud  
**Phase:** Requirements Gathering

## 1. Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder Group | Role | Interest | Influence | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project Team | Develops and maintains the platform, including web scraping, data processing, and the Data Warehouse. | High | High | Clear requirements, reliable data sources, automated pipelines, data quality checks, and a maintainable architecture. |
| Business Intelligence (BI) Users | Analyze news coverage using dashboards, KPIs, and analytical reports. | High | High | Accurate KPIs, interactive dashboards, comparisons between news sources, and historical trends. |
| Egyptian News Websites | Provide news articles and publication metadata collected by the platform. | Medium | High | Responsible data collection, compliance with applicable website terms and access restrictions, and appropriate handling of published content. |
| General News Readers | Read news on the original news websites and may indirectly benefit from the insights generated by the platform. | Medium | Low | Reliable analysis of news coverage, clear comparisons, and insights that help them understand how different sources cover similar stories. |

**Note:** Interest and influence levels are preliminary assessments and should be validated with the project supervisor. General News Readers are indirect stakeholders unless the platform provides them with a direct interface.

## 2. Stakeholder Needs and Related KPIs

| Stakeholder | Specific Need | Related KPI / Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Project Team | Monitor pipeline execution and identify scraping failures. | Pipeline Success Rate (%), Scraping Failure Rate (%) |
| Project Team | Ensure that collected data is complete and usable. | Data Completeness (%), Duplicate Article Rate (%) |
| BI Users | Compare the volume of news published by different websites. | Number of Articles per Source, Source Share (%) |
| BI Users | Identify stories covered by multiple news websites. | Number of Sources Covering the Same or Similar Story |
| BI Users | Compare publication timing across different sources. | Publication Time Difference Between Sources |
| Egyptian News Websites | Ensure articles are attributed to their original sources. | Number of Collected Articles per Source, Source Attribution Completeness (%) |
| General News Readers | Understand differences in news coverage across sources. | Number of Sources Covering the Same Story, Publication Time Differences, Coverage Distribution |

**Note:** These KPIs are proposed measurements. Identifying the same or similar stories requires a suitable story-matching method, while pipeline monitoring requires execution logs or monitoring records.

## 3. Candidate Actor List for Use Cases

Not every stakeholder must be an actor in the Use Case Diagram. An actor is a person or external system that directly interacts with the platform.

| Candidate Actor | Type | Interaction with the Platform |
|---|---|---|
| BI User / Data Analyst | Human Actor | Views dashboards, filters reports, compares news sources, and analyzes KPIs. |
| System Administrator / Project Team Member | Human Actor | Monitors pipeline execution, reviews errors, and manages system operations, if these functions are supported. |
| Scheduler | Supporting System Actor | Triggers scraping and data-processing jobs automatically. |
| Egyptian News Websites | External Systems / Data Sources | Provide articles and metadata through web scraping, subject to applicable access restrictions. |

**General News Readers:** They are not included as direct actors in the current version because the project overview focuses on collecting and analyzing news data rather than providing a direct news-reading interface.

The Project Team should not necessarily be represented as a single actor. Its responsibilities may be divided into specific roles, such as System Administrator and Data Engineer, depending on the final system functionality.

## 4. Stakeholder Needs and Initial User Stories

The following user stories provide initial inputs for Task 7 – User Stories & Use Cases.

**User Story 1 – Compare News Sources**

As a BI user, I want to compare article counts across Egyptian news websites so that I can identify differences in news coverage.

**User Story 2 – Identify Shared News Stories**

As a BI user, I want to identify stories covered by multiple news sources so that I can analyze how different websites cover similar events.

**User Story 3 – Compare Publication Times**

As a BI user, I want to compare publication dates and times across news sources so that I can examine differences in reporting timelines.

**User Story 4 – Monitor Pipeline Execution**

As a system maintainer, I want to monitor scheduled pipeline executions so that I can detect and investigate failures.

**User Story 5 – Maintain Data Quality**

As a data engineer, I want to validate and standardize collected articles so that the Data Warehouse contains consistent and reliable data.
